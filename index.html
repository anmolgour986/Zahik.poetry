The Poet

A Life Written in Verses

Z.D.

Zahik Abas Dar is a poet whose verses live at the intersection of memory, longing, and the sacred. Born into the rich literary soil of the subcontinent, his work draws from classical Urdu tradition while speaking to the restlessness of the modern soul.

His poetry does not merely describe — it inhabits. Each line is a dwelling place, an interior architecture of feeling that readers enter and rarely leave unchanged. He writes in Urdu, Kashmiri, and occasionally English, slipping between languages as one slips between moods.

Beyond poetry, Zahik Abas Dar is a scholar of classical literature, a collector of forgotten manuscripts, and a quiet participant in literary gatherings where words are spoken with reverence and listened to with closed eyes.

Selected Verses

Words That Outlast the Moment

On Longing

The river remembers every stone
it has ever touched —
I remember you the way water
remembers its first thirst.

دریا ہر پتھر کو یاد رکھتا ہے
جسے اس نے چھوا ہو —
میں تمہیں اس طرح یاد کرتا ہوں
جیسے پانی اپنی پہلی پیاس کو

On Silence

Between one word and the next
lives an entire city —
I have built my home
in that pause.

ایک لفظ اور دوسرے لفظ کے درمیان
ایک پورا شہر بستا ہے —
میں نے اپنا گھر
اسی توقف میں بنایا ہے

On Time

Mornings do not arrive —
they peel themselves
from the darkness
like old letters from an envelope.

صبحیں آتی نہیں —
وہ خود کو اندھیرے سے
ادھیڑ لیتی ہیں
جیسے پرانے خطوط لفافے سے

On Home

I carry my homeland
the way the blind carry light —
not in the eyes,
but in the warmth of the face.

میں اپنا وطن اٹھائے پھرتا ہوں
جیسے اندھے روشنی لے کر چلتے ہیں —
آنکھوں میں نہیں،
چہرے کی گرمی میں

"A poem is not written — it is excavated, the way a river excavates its own bed, slowly, over years, with the patience of someone who has nowhere else to go."
— Zahik Abas Dar

The Journey

Milestones in Words

Early Years

First Verses

Began writing poetry as a young student, captivated by the classical ghazal tradition and the rich oral storytelling culture of the region.

Formative Period

Literary Immersion

Studied classical Urdu and Persian literature, drawing influence from Mir, Ghalib, and Faiz. Began participating in mushairas and literary circles.

Debut

First Published Collection

Released his debut anthology of Urdu poems, gaining recognition for the lyrical depth and emotional precision of his verse.

Recognition

Awards & Fellowships

Received literary honours from regional cultural academies, and was invited as a featured poet at several national literary festivals.

Present

Ongoing Work

Continues writing, mentoring young poets, and contributing to the preservation and promotion of classical Urdu literary traditions.
